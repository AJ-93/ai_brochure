# Hugging Face: The AI Community Building the Future

The platform where the machine learning community collaborates on models, datasets, and applications. A home for open-source ML, collaboration, and ethical AI development.

---

## What we offer

- Models, Datasets, Spaces, and Buckets
  - Browse 2M+ models and 500k+ datasets
  - Spaces to run and share AI apps
  - Storage Buckets for organizing and hosting work
- A thriving, open ecosystem
  - Move faster with the HF Open Source Stack
  - Host and collaborate on unlimited public models, datasets, and applications
- Multiple entry points for developers and teams
  - HuggingFace PRO and Team & Enterprise options
  - Enterprise Support, Inference Providers, Inference Endpoints
  - HuggingChat for conversational AI experiences

 Quick stats from our platform:
  - Models: 2M+ and counting
  - Datasets: 500k+ documented
  - Spaces: a growing set of AI apps and demos
  - Applications: 1M+ and growing
